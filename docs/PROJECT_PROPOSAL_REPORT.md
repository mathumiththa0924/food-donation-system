# Project Proposal and Report

## 1. Project Title
A Web-Based Food Donation and Redistribution System

## 2. Introduction
Food wastage is a major global issue affecting both developed and developing countries. Every day, a large amount of food is wasted by restaurants, hotels, supermarkets, and households due to overproduction, poor planning, or lack of proper storage. This wasted food could otherwise be used to feed millions of people who suffer from hunger and malnutrition.

In many urban areas, especially in developing countries, there is a noticeable imbalance between food surplus and food scarcity. While some communities have excess food, others struggle to meet their daily nutritional needs. This imbalance is mainly due to the absence of an efficient system to redistribute surplus food.

Currently, food donation is often handled through informal or manual channels such as phone calls, personal contacts, or small local initiatives. These approaches are inefficient, time-consuming, and unreliable. There is no centralized digital platform that connects food donors with NGOs, volunteers, and needy communities in a structured and efficient way.

Therefore, a modern web-based solution is needed to bridge this gap by connecting food donors with those in need, ensuring that surplus food is utilized effectively instead of being wasted.

## 3. Problem Statement

### Who faces the problem
- Restaurants and hotels that generate excess food
- Households that occasionally have surplus food
- NGOs and charity organizations that need food resources
- Volunteers who want to help distribute food
- Needy individuals and communities suffering from hunger

### What the problem is
There is no centralized digital system that allows donors to easily share information about available surplus food and enables NGOs or volunteers to access and collect it efficiently. The lack of coordination leads to food wastage and missed opportunities for helping people.

### Why it needs to be solved
- To reduce food wastage and environmental impact
- To improve food accessibility for underprivileged communities
- To create an organized and transparent system for food donation
- To save time and effort for both donors and NGOs
- To promote social responsibility and community support

## 4. Objectives

### General Objective
To design and develop a web-based platform that efficiently connects food donors with NGOs and volunteers for effective food redistribution.

### Specific Objectives
- To implement a secure user authentication system with role-based access (Admin, Donor, NGO)
- To enable donors to easily post details of surplus food including quantity, type, expiry time, and location
- To allow NGOs and volunteers to browse, filter, and request available food donations
- To implement a real-time status tracking system (Pending, Picked Up, Delivered)
- To develop dashboards that provide useful analytics such as total donations and completed deliveries
- To include a feedback and rating system to improve trust and reliability
- To provide a simple and user-friendly interface for all types of users

## 5. Scope of the System

### In Scope
- User registration and login using secure authentication (JWT)
- Role-Based Access Control (Admin, Donor, NGO/Volunteer)
- Food donation posting system with details (food type, quantity, expiry, location)
- Viewing and filtering food posts based on location
- Request and acceptance system for NGOs
- Pickup and delivery workflow management
- Status tracking system (Pending → Picked Up → Delivered)
- Notification system for updates (basic level)
- Feedback and rating system
- Dashboard with analytics (donations, pickups, deliveries)
- Responsive web design

### Out of Scope
- Online payment integration
- Real-time GPS tracking system
- Native mobile application development
- AI-based food demand prediction
- Integration with external government or NGO systems

## 6. Proposed Solution

### Type of System
This project will be developed as a web-based full stack application using the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, Node.js).

### Key Features
- Secure login system with role-based dashboards
- Food donation posting and management
- NGO request and acceptance workflow
- Pickup and delivery tracking system
- Location-based filtering (city-level)
- Notification system for updates
- Feedback and rating system
- Analytics dashboard for insights

### How it solves the problem
The system acts as a centralized platform that connects food donors with NGOs and volunteers. Donors can easily post surplus food, and NGOs can quickly respond and arrange pickups. This ensures that food is redistributed efficiently, reducing waste and helping those in need.
